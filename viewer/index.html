Known issues in the prototype

  1. A bus edge can be counted as drawn without being drawn when its section is collapsed and it is alone in its bundle.
  2. The provenance key can disappear. It closes itself on narrow screens (under 760 px of map). It should shrink to a strip, never vanish.
  3. The layout is still a placeholder: sections are placed in simple columns, with no attempt to shorten edges.
  4. Not drawn yet: missing files, the generates and embeds edge kinds, file roles, focus lenses.

Things worth trying

  • New: How it works. On the real HORDE crawl, switch the view to How it works. Two stories, "One audio block" and "A note arrives", explain what the code does step by step; every step cites file and line. Click a step to rename it, pin it as truth, add or delete steps, or leave the agent a redraw request. Your edits stay in this browser until you send them (Copy edits).
  • Operational lens. On the real HORDE crawl, press Lens: Operational. Only what is reachable from the plugin's entry points stays (38 files); everything else folds into one counted box. Click the box to list every crossing edge. The inspector lists leaks (none on HORDE), 11 product-named files nothing ships with, and build-time generators.
  • Right-click. Right-click a section, file, fold row or port for its actions. On a port, "Isolate" walks you to that section, sub-patch to sub-patch.
  • Back and forward. The ← → buttons, ⌘Z / ⇧⌘Z (Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y), or your mouse's side buttons step through what you did. Hover a button to see the step it would undo.
  • Fold. On the real HORDE crawl, tools shows its 12 busiest files and a dashed +108 more row. Click the row to show all; select the section and use "Fold again" to put it back.
  • Isolate. Select a section (try src) and press Isolate. Inputs sit on the left rail, outputs on the right, one port per section; click a port to list its files. Esc or "Back to full map" returns.
  • Collapse a section, then expand it: the meter should stay balanced and the counts should match.
  • Zoom out to overview and back: nothing should vanish that the meter still counts.
  • Narrow the window: the provenance key should stay on screen (issue 3 says it doesn't yet).
  • Switch Flow and Design: same nodes, same edges, only arrangement changes.
  • On the real HORDE crawl, read the section list on the right against what you know of HORDE.
