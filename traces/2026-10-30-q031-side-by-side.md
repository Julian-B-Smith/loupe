    the matrix stay in Both; switching view or going Back across it refits both cameras.

    selection's screen rect). A stand-in holding a selected file or edge end is marked: a box zoomed out or collapsed,
    a fold row, a port, the lens's outside box. Found while building: zoomed out, a selected file marked nothing in
    Design, which is where a half-width pane lands at fit. This also changes the single Design view. `zoom.padTop`: in
    Both each pane's meter stands over its top-left corner; the fit and the matrix's title band keep below it (in the
    browser the matrix bar, pushed down by the meter, covered the title), and under 900 px the subtitle wraps.
  - `index.html`: the two panes (the main pane keeps the one-pane views and the transition, and is Design in Both),
    the divider (drag or arrow keys, 20 to 80 percent, kept in this browser), a meter per pane, pointer, wheel and zoom

    Flow draws) reads Flow's frame (`tagPane`); a selected file the isolate puts elsewhere marks the isolate bar.
