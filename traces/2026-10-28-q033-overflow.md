- **Julian (poll, 2026-10-28):** an inline lane past 12 steps keeps its first and last 5, the middle files folding into

- **Change:**

    with one provenance merge: a comb of ticks along the files, one tap line to the rail through a gap between file
    rows (a different gap per section, 9 px apart), a count pill by the comb, `data-taps`. The edges stay `via: 'bus'`;
    `tap` names the merged tap, which opens to them (`data-holds="m:<bus>|<section>|<prov>|<side>"`, inspect.js
    `heldEdges`, the inspector heading "Merged tap from …").
  - Lane (layout.js `laneFoldAbove`, `laneKeep`; render.js fold rows and spine): the middle of an inline lane is a fold
    keyed by the lane id (`layout.folds[lane]`, `lane: true`), proxied, opening to its edges, unfolded by
    `state.unfolded`, moving with its section; discs keep their step's own number; lines off counts it in the lane's
    section. Routes are untouched.
  - Twins (render.js): past 4 ports one chip per twin (`data-twin`, `data-conf`); hovering it (`state.hover.twin`,
    index.html hit test, state.js `same`) draws the grid last, over everything, inside the pane.
  - Stress cases: big-bus has five banks of ten voices (bank E: 2 runtime edges); long-lane has 16 steps inline and 16
    routed. docs/design-handoff.md's table says so.
