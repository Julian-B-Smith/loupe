# q028-open-all — every element that stands for several edges opens to them

- **Queue item:** Q-028 (ADR 0029, the rest of its first half; a P2 gate, required before v1: Julian, 2026-10-27).
- **Change:** `design/prototype/render.js` records, on every routed edge's meter entry, the element keys it is drawn
  between (`ends`); fold rows, closed ports (file ports too), the elsewhere chip carry their shown count; the
  overview's loose block becomes clickable; a bus rail label carries its count. `inspect.js` `heldEdges` lists an
  element's edges from that record. `index.html`: the right-click menu lists "the N edges ..." on fold rows, ports,
  the elsewhere chip, the lens box, and any box that replaces its contents (collapsed, or in the overview); every
  element keeps its own left click; the bus panel splits "on this rail" from members routed another way; the panel
  is not rebuilt on a hover when nothing it lists changed.
- **Decision (lead, from ADR 0029's "a counted box"):** an expanded box does not stand for edges: it draws them,
  and its ×N lines open (Q-011). Recorded in ROADMAP Q-028.
- **Evidence:** `tools/render.test.mjs` over 5 snapshots: 4 zooms with and without the lens, each top isolated, each
  isolated with its section ports open, collapsed tops, a collapsed child alone and inside its collapsed parent. 595
  elements, 749 recorded ends, 50 bus frames: each element's shown count equals its list; each listed edge touches the
  element by graph data and layout; a collapsed box, or a top box in the overview, holds every edge touching its
  files (not a subset); each bus rail's count equals the meter's; every routed edge records its ends. Plants, each
  failing: fold row, port and elsewhere miscounts; an edge credited to the wrong fold; a bus misattributed; a
  collapsed box without ends; an open port keeping its count; a rail counting every member; a box under-listing.
- **Critic (opus): FIX FIRST**, all fixed: H1 a file port's left click had stopped selecting its file; H2 an open
  section port showed a count and held nothing (the test never opened a port); H3 the bus test filtered out the
  mismatch it was for (horde-sample's audio bus: the panel said 5, the rail 4); H4 expanded boxes offered a list
  that described them wrongly; M1 the panel went stale across zooms and open ports; M2 under-listing passed; M3 the
  lens box now lists, the story-outside box is Q-032; L1 tooltips say right-click lists; L3 no rebuild on hover; L4 a
  comment overclaimed. Not changed: no highlight of a selected box or port (L2).
- **Found on the way:** a template-literal slip in `index.html` broke the whole viewer while `./verify fast` stayed
