# 0017. Design delivers a working prototype

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-09-30
- **Source:** Julian, to the design agent: its job is the design system plus a visual prototype he can wire
  into the logic. Amends 0013.

## Consequences

- The design agent builds a prototype in `design/prototype/` that renders real snapshot files (`graph.json`,
  `overlay.json`, `view.json`) and reads its styling from tokens. It replaces the screens-canvas deliverable.
- `render.js` holds no structural logic; grouping, bundling and membership come from `view.json`.
  `layout.js` is replaceable by the infrastructure layout engine.
- Write lanes: design writes `design/`; infrastructure writes `schema/`, `tools/`, `docs/`.
- The handoff packet is version 2 (`docs/design-handoff.md`), which also answers the design agent's 18
  review points.

## Addendum (2026-09-30, packet v2.1)

- The design agent works in Claude Design and cannot write into the Loupe folder. It receives files through
  the Loupe data kit artifact and delivers its prototype as an artifact; infrastructure copies it into
  `design/prototype/`.
- Its v2.1 defaults are accepted: `../tokens.css` generated by infrastructure, component folders named by
  exact id, soft diff fills, interaction precedence selected > hover > related, exemptions never recolor
  edges, dash patterns that differ in kind, the arrowhead mapping, and `edge-7` as a kind slot.
